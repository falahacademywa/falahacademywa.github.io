Islamic Studies

Knowledge and character grounded in the Qur'an and Sunnah — through the MESBA curriculum.

وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

"And say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

— Surah Ta-Ha (20:114)

Faith, Knowledge & Character

Our Islamic Studies program uses the MESBA curriculum — a structured, level-based framework used by full-time Islamic schools across North America. Children learn the beliefs, practices, history and manners of Islam in an age-appropriate sequence that deepens each year, so that knowledge grows alongside akhlaq (character) and a living connection to Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.

MESBA curriculum Level-based, ages ~5–15 Arabic language & vocabulary Character & manners

What Children Learn

The MESBA framework covers the core areas of Islamic knowledge, revisited at a deeper level each year as the child advances.

'Aqidah — Beliefs

The foundations of faith: belief in Allah, His angels, books, messengers, the Last Day, and divine decree.

Fiqh — Worship

How to practice Islam correctly: purification, salah, sawm, zakah and the etiquette of worship.

Ahadith — Sayings of the Prophet ﷺ

Selected authentic ahadith with their meanings and lessons for daily life.

Sirah — The Prophet's Life ﷺ

The life, character and example of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ as a model to love and follow.

Tarikh — Islamic History

The stories of the prophets and the great figures and events of Islamic history.

Akhlaq — Character

Islamic character and good conduct — honesty, kindness, patience, and the values of a believer.

Adab — Manners & Du'as

The manners and etiquette of daily life, and the du'as (supplications) for everyday occasions.

How We Teach It

Level by level

Each topic returns every year with more depth, matched to the child's age and stage.

Knowledge to action

What's learned in class is practiced in the school day — salah, manners, and du'as lived daily.

Arabic alongside

Key vocabulary and terms in Arabic, connecting studies back to the Qur'an itself.
