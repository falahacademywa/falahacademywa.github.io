The Falah Academy app

Your Family Portal, on your phone's Home Screen

Free · nothing to buy, no app store
  • 🔔School alerts on your phone — announcements, class updates and fee receipts arrive as notifications.
  • 🩺Your child's profile — see and update contact details, medical information and allergies.
  • 📈Progress — attendance, Qur'an notes and the teachers' class updates.
  • 📅Academic calendar — holidays, breaks and school events, and add them to your phone's calendar.
  • 💳Fees and receipts — what is paid, what is due, and receipts to download.

Install in 1 minute

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open the Family Portal below.
  3. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow ⬆), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Falah from your Home Screen, sign in, and tap Turn on for notifications.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Open the Family Portal below.
  3. Tap Install on the blue banner (or in Chrome's ⋮ menu, Add to Home screen → Install).
  4. Open Falah from your Home Screen, sign in, and tap Turn on for notifications.
