Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Falah Academy is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 39-2254583). Keep your Zelle or bank record, and we will send a written acknowledgement for your taxes.

How do I set up a monthly Zelle?

In your banking app open Zelle, choose falahacademywa@gmail.com, enter the amount, and pick "Repeat" or "Schedule recurring" before you confirm. Set it monthly and the sadaqah continues automatically.

Does my employer match donations?

Search your company intranet for "matching gifts" or "giving portal", or ask HR. If your company uses Benevity, search for cause ID 840-392254583. Your donation and the match both reach the school, and the portal issues your receipt.
