Our Curriculum Partners

We bring the best of established, accredited curricula into a full-time Islamic school.

Rather than reinvent every subject, Falah Academy partners with trusted, established providers for our Islamic studies, accredited academics and STEM — then delivers them together with our own Qur'an program in one nurturing, full-time Islamic day. Every partner was chosen for quality, recognition, and a good fit with our values.

MESBA

Islamic Studies Curriculum

MESBA provides a structured, level-based Islamic studies curriculum used by full-time Islamic schools across North America. It covers beliefs, worship, the life of the Prophet ﷺ, Islamic history, character and manners — organized so that learning deepens each year as the child advances.

Level-based, ages ~5–15Beliefs · Fiqh · SirahHistory · Character

Red Comet

Accredited Academics (Washington ALE)

Red Comet is an accredited online education provider that powers our core academic subjects — English, Math, Science and Social Studies — under Washington State's Alternative Learning Experience framework. Its coursework is standards-aligned and its records are recognized and transferable.

Cognia accreditedNWAC accreditedOSPI / WA ALE

iCode

STEM & Coding

iCode delivers a hands-on, project-based STEM curriculum — robotics, coding, drones and AI — organized into a belt system that lets students advance and see their progress. It brings real technology skills and computational thinking into the Falah day.

Project-based10-belt progressionRobotics · Coding · AI
