Accredited Academics

A full, Washington-recognized academic education — English, Math, Science and Social Studies — delivered through our accredited partner, Red Comet.

Real Academics, Fully Accredited

Falah Academy delivers its core academic subjects through Red Comet, an accredited online school provider, under Washington State's Alternative Learning Experience (ALE) framework. This means your child receives a rigorous, standards-aligned education — with coursework, grades and records that are recognized and transferable — while learning in a nurturing, full-time Islamic environment.

Cognia accredited NWAC accredited OSPI / Washington ALE Standards-aligned Transferable records

Accreditations shown are those of Red Comet, Falah Academy's accredited academic provider, whose standards-aligned coursework we deliver under Washington's Alternative Learning Experience program.
