Every child grows across four programs each day — reading the Qur'an, deep Islamic studies, Washington-accredited academics, and hands-on STEM. Explore each below.

From the first letter of the Qaida to reading the whole Mushaf — a structured five-year plan with Tajweed, fluency, and love for the Book of Allah.
