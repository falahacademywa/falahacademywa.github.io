STEM & Coding

Robotics, coding, drones and AI — hands-on technology education through our partner, iCode.

Tomorrow's Skills, Today

Through our partnership with iCode, Falah students explore science, technology, engineering and math with real tools and real projects. Children learn computational thinking — how to break down problems, design solutions and build them — through a hands-on, project-based curriculum that grows with them. It's an education that pairs the timeless with the cutting-edge: the Qur'an in the morning, robotics and code in the same building.

Robotics

Building and programming robots to complete real challenges.

Coding

From block-based coding to real programming languages, step by step.

Drones

Flight, sensors and the basics of how autonomous machines work.

AI & Emerging Tech

An age-appropriate introduction to how artificial intelligence works.

Engineering & Design

Designing, testing and improving — the engineering mindset.

Problem Solving

Logic, creativity and teamwork that reach far beyond the screen.
