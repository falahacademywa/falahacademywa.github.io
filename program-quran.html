Qur'an Reading

From the first letter to the whole Mushaf — a structured journey in reading the Book of Allah with correct Tajweed.

وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا

"And recite the Qur'an with measured recitation."

— Surah Al-Muzzammil (73:4)

A Reading Journey, Not a Race

At Falah Academy, learning to read the Qur'an is the heart of every school day. Children begin with the Noorani Qaida — the letters, their sounds, and the rules of joining them — and move, step by step, toward reading the Qur'an fluently and beautifully. Our goal is not speed but correct, confident recitation with Tajweed, built on a foundation that lasts a lifetime, insha'Allah.

Recognition

Every letter, its shapes, and its sound — mastered before moving on.

Tajweed

The rules of pronunciation taught from the very first lessons.

Fluency

Joining letters into words, then words into flowing recitation.

Love

A gentle, encouraging environment that builds a lasting bond with the Qur'an.

The Journey Ahead — Pre-K to Grade 4

As Falah grows one grade each year, here is the reading pathway a child follows — from their first Qaida lesson toward completing the recitation of the Qur'an. Each stage builds on the one before it.

PRE-K & KG Noorani Qaida The Foundation Arabic letters, their sounds and shapes, harakat, and joining letters — with Tajweed introduced from the start.
GRADE 1 Qaida to Qur'an Juz 'Amma begins Completing the Qaida and beginning to read short surahs from the 30th Juz with support.
GRADE 2 Fluent Reading Reading from the Mushaf Reading directly from the Qur'an with growing fluency and applied Tajweed rules.
GRADE 3 Tajweed Applied Consistent recitation Refining pronunciation, rhythm and the rules of stopping (waqf) across longer passages.
GRADE 4 Confident Reciter Reading the whole Mushaf Reading any part of the Qur'an fluently and correctly — the goal of the reading journey, insha'Allah.

How We Teach

A simple, proven method repeated daily until it becomes second nature.

1

Listen

The teacher recites; children hear correct pronunciation and Tajweed modeled clearly.

2

Repeat

Children echo together and one by one, correcting sounds gently as they go.

3

Read

Each child reads their lesson aloud to the teacher for individual feedback.

4

Revise

Daily review locks in what was learned before moving to the next lesson.

"The best among you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it." — Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Sahih al-Bukhari)

What Makes It Work

  • Daily practice. Qur'an reading is part of every school day — consistency is what builds fluent readers.
  • Small, focused attention. Every child reads to the teacher individually, so no one is left behind or held back.
  • Tajweed from day one. Correct pronunciation is built in from the first letter, not corrected later.
  • A joyful atmosphere. Encouragement, patience, and celebration of every milestone build a lasting love for the Qur'an.
  • Parents kept in the loop. Progress and Qur'an updates reach families through the Family Portal.
